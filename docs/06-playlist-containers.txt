PLAYLIST CONTAINERS AND SEQUENCES
=================================

A playlist plays until it ends.  A container plays one or more
playlists for a set time, then moves on.  A sequence plays several
containers in order.

Uses
----

  * Timed segments: for example 10 minutes of quiet music, 30 minutes
    of fast music, 10 minutes of quiet music.  Each part is a
    container and the three are a sequence; play stops at the end.
  * Background music by the clock: one style for an hour, then another.
  * A segment built from several playlists, played in order.
  * Ending a dynamic playlist.  A dynamic playlist refills itself and
    never ends; a container gives it an end.  A container with a
    dynamic playlist must have a time limit.
  * A sleep timer: a container with a 30 minute limit and fade out.

Parts
-----

  Playlist (static or dynamic)   holds songs
  Container                      holds playlists, a time limit and an
                                 end mode
  Sequence                       holds containers

A container or a sequence can be played.  A container cannot hold a
container and a sequence cannot hold a sequence.

In the list, a container shows "(10:00, 2 playlists)" and a sequence
"(3 sections, 50:00)": the limit or total (m:ss or h:mm:ss).  The
total is omitted if any section has no limit.

Containers and sequences have their own name spaces: a container may
have the same name as a playlist; two containers may not share a name.

Creating a container
--------------------

Playlists tab > New container...

  Name                  required
  Time limit            check the box and set minutes and seconds, at
                        least one second; unchecked means no limit
  At the limit          fade out, then go on (default)
                        cut off, then go on
                        let the track finish, then go on
  If the playlists run out
                        start the playlists again (default)
                        go on to the next section early
  Playlists             Add... picks static or dynamic playlists (each
                        once); Remove, Move up, Move down

Edit... changes a selected container.

Creating a sequence
-------------------

New sequence...  Enter a name and Add... containers.  A container may
be added more than once.  Move up and Move down set the order.  At
least one container is required.  Edit... changes it.

Playing
-------

In the player, select the container or sequence and click Play.
Shuffle randomizes the songs within each section.

  * The clock counts only time during which music plays; pausing stops
    it.
  * A section's songs are those of its playlists in order (a dynamic
    playlist contributes its current list).
  * At the limit:
      fade    the volume falls to zero over 3 seconds, then the next
              section starts
      cut     the music stops and the next section starts
      finish  the current song ends, then the next section starts
  * If the songs run out before the limit, the section either starts
    its playlists again or goes on early, per its setting.
  * A section with no songs is skipped with a message.
  * After the last section playback stops ("Sequence complete.").  A
    container played alone stops when its time is used.

A line shows the section, its position, and the time left:

    Cool down (3/3) - 4:12 left - 4:12 in all

Playback > Next section (Ctrl+Shift+Right) goes to the next section.

Editing a playlist in a container changes what the container plays the
next time it starts.  Deleting a playlist removes it from containers;
deleting a container removes it from sequences.

Phone
-----

The phone lists sequences, containers and playlists in the Playlists
tab and plays them with the same limits and end modes.  Create and
edit them on the desktop and sync.

Storage
-------

A container or sequence is a row in the playlist table with its kind,
limit, end mode and "when empty" setting.  A container's playlists and
a sequence's containers are rows in playlist_member, in order.
