SEARCHING ON THE PHONE
======================

The Songs tab lists every song in the library (duplicates are not
listed) and has a search box in the top bar.  The placeholder reads
"Artist, album, song or genre (N)", where N is the number of songs in the
library.

Matching
--------

The list shows the songs that match every word typed.

  * Each word must match the start of a word in the song's title,
    artist, album or genre, taken together.  "dio" matches "Dio" and
    "Dion", not "Audio".
  * The order of the words does not matter, and the words may match
    different fields: "dio holy" finds "Holy Diver" by Dio.
  * Capitals are ignored.
  * Punctuation counts as a space: "rock&roll", "rock/roll" and
    "rock roll" are the same search.  Accents are not removed.
  * A song's genre is all of its genres.  A song with the genres
    "Rock; Blues" is found by "rock" and by "blues".
  * Only the ARTIST tag is searched, not the album artist.
  * A word matches at the start of a word only: "star" finds "Star
    Wars" and "Death Star", not "Mustard".

Searching one field
-------------------

Without a prefix a word is looked for in all four fields, so "classical"
also finds the song "Classical Gas".  A prefix limits a word to one
field: title, artist, album, genre or path (the folder and file name).

    genre:classical    songs whose genre starts a word with "classical",
                       not "Classical Gas" by an artist of another genre
    artist:dio         songs by Dio, not songs titled "Dio" or from a
                       tribute album
    album:"the wall"   a phrase, in the album only
    =rock genre:=rock  whole words only: "rock", not "rockabilly"

  * Quotes ("the wall") match words in that order, side by side.
  * A leading "=" asks for the whole word: "=dio" finds Dio, not Dion.
  * Text before a colon that is not one of the five names is searched
    for as typed.
  * Prefixes and plain words combine, and every test must match.

Examples:

    miles kind       songs with a word starting "miles" and a word
                     starting "kind" in title, artist, album or genre
    work             songs with the genre Work (and any other song with
                     a word starting "work"; see 05 for genres used as
                     labels)
    rock blues       songs with both words

The list updates 200 ms after typing stops.  If nothing matches, the
list shows "Nothing matches".  Clear the box to see all songs.

Sorting
-------

The button next to the search box sets the order: Artist (the default,
then album, track number and title), Title, Album (then track number)
or Folder (the file's path).  The search applies to the sorted list.

Playing from a result
---------------------

Tap a song to play it.  The songs in the list as it is shown (filtered
and sorted) become the queue: Next plays the song below, and shuffle
shuffles that list.  Tapping the song that is already playing does not
restart it or change the queue.

Search applies to the Songs tab only.  The Playlists tab has no search.
