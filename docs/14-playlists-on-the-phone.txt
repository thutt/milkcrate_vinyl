PLAYLISTS ON THE PHONE
======================

The Playlists tab shows the playlists made on the desktop: static
playlists (04), dynamic playlists (05), containers and sequences (06).
The phone plays them; it cannot create, rename, edit or delete them.
Make changes on the desktop and sync (11).

The app opens on the Playlists tab when the library has playlists.  The
tab title shows the number of playlists, and a note symbol while the
music comes from one of them.

The list
--------

Three groups, each in alphabetical order: Sequences, Containers and
Playlists (static and dynamic together).  A row shows:

    name
    details

Details by kind:

    static playlist    "42 tracks"
    dynamic playlist   "20 tracks  .  dynamic  .  1530 matched"
    container          "10:00, fades out  .  playlist names"
                       (fades out, cut off or finishes the track)
    sequence           "3 containers: container names"

"N matched" is the number of library songs that the dynamic playlist's
rules select (05).  A playing row is tinted and its details begin with
"Playing" or "Paused".

Each row has a play button.  The marked row (grey tint) is the one the
main play button starts when nothing is playing; it is the first one to
begin with, and the last row tapped afterwards.

Opening and playing
-------------------

  * Tap a static or dynamic playlist: its songs are shown.  The top bar
    shows the name and, for a dynamic playlist, the "N matched" count;
    the arrow returns to the list.  Tap a song to play from it.  Under
    each song of a dynamic playlist a line gives the values its rules
    test, such as "Last played (any copy): never . Rating: not rated",
    to show why it was chosen (05).
  * Tap a container or sequence: it plays.
  * The play button on a row plays that playlist from its start, or
    from a random song if shuffle is on.
  * The main play button, with nothing playing: on the list, it plays
    the marked playlist; in an opened playlist, it plays the first song.

Playing a playlist makes its songs the queue.  Next, Previous and
shuffle work within it.  Shuffle (the button beside the controls) plays
in random order without repeating a song until all have played.
A dynamic playlist always plays in its own order, and so does a
container or sequence that holds one: while it plays, the shuffle button
is dimmed and says "in order", and a play from the list starts at its
first song.  The shuffle choice comes back when anything else plays.  The
desktop player does the same (the Shuffle button of the Playlists tab and
Random order are off for such a list).

Tapping the song that is playing does not restart it.

Dynamic playlists
-----------------

A song counts as played after 20 seconds of listening.  When a counted
song ends or is left, the dynamic playlist drops it and adds a matching
song at the end, as on the desktop (05).  The change reaches the
desktop at the next sync.

Playing a dynamic playlist, the app takes each next song from the list as
the database holds it at that moment, not from a copy made at the start.
What the screen shows as coming up is therefore what plays.  In order,
the next song is the first one after the current song's place in the
list; with shuffle on it is any song of the list not yet played.  A song
is played once in a round, whether it was heard or skipped.  Next on the
last song of the list is a skip like any other: the song leaves the list
when a later song counts, and the list starts again from its first song
after a few seconds.  Changes to
the list meanwhile (replacements, a sync) take effect with the next
song.  When no song is left, the app reads the list again after a few
seconds, once the replacements are in, and starts a new round.  If the
list has no songs, the app stays open, paused, and waits; the
notification and the player controls stay; Play starts the list again
when it has songs.  The app does not stop itself at the end of a list.

Containers and sequences
------------------------

While one plays, a line above the controls shows the section, its
position in the sequence, the time left and the total time left, with a
"Next section" button.  The limits and end modes (fade, cut, finish)
and the "when empty" setting work as on the desktop (06).  Playback
stops after the last section.

Back button
-----------

  * In an opened playlist: returns to the list of playlists.
  * In the list of playlists: goes to the Songs tab.
  * In the Songs tab: hides the app, as Home does.  Music keeps
    playing.  Swiping the app away from recent apps stops it.

Notification and lock screen
----------------------------

Controls for play/pause, next, previous, stop, seek and the rating
buttons (13).

The line under the song's name holds the artist, the rating and the
time left: "Queen  .  4 *  .  -3:42" (a middle dot and the star symbol on
the phone).  Songs over an hour show "-1:03:42".  The text is sent when
a song starts, when you pause, seek or change the rating, and every 10
seconds while the music plays, so it can be up to 10 seconds behind.
The progress bar needs no updates and moves by itself.  When the length
of the song is not known yet, the time is left out.  The same text goes
to the car's display, if the car shows the artist line.

Many head units show the title, the artist and the album, one line
each, and cannot show more.  The artist and the album are sent as they
are.  The title line alternates every 10 seconds between the song's
title and the rating with the time left ("4 *  .  -3:42"); each song
starts with its title, and a paused player shows the title.

Updating the playlists
----------------------

Playlists on the phone are the ones in its copy of tags.db.  After a
change on the desktop, sync (11); the list is reloaded when the app
returns to the foreground.
