RATING ON THE PHONE
===================

The phone rates the song that is playing, and the songs that were played
lately (see the end).  Ratings are explained in
07-song-rating.txt: unrated and 0 stars are different values.

Where the rating is shown
-------------------------

  * In the song list, the playing song's row is larger and shows its
    rating below the artist.
  * Every other row in the song list and in a playlist shows its stars
    (filled for the rating, outlined for the rest) at the right; a song
    that is not rated shows none.  A rating set on the phone shows in
    the lists at once; ratings from the desktop show after a sync.
  * Each row of the song list has a line of its own: "Plays: 12  .  Last
    played: 3 days ago".  The plays and the date are those of the whole
    song, every copy with the same artist and title (05).  They are
    worked out in the background after the library is read and after a
    song is counted, and a row shows them when it comes onto the screen.
  * When the playing song's row is not on the screen, the rating is
    shown in the control panel at the bottom, below the song's name.
  * On the lock screen and in the notification, the line under the
    song shows the rating as a number and a star ("4 *" in this text;
    the phone shows the star symbol), or "unrated".

Display:

    unrated     the word "unrated", no stars
    rated       a circle with the number of stars (0 to 5), and five
                stars, filled for the rating

In the app
----------

  * Tap "unrated": the song becomes 0 stars.
  * Tap star N: the song gets N stars.  Tapping the star that is
    already the rating sets 0 stars.
  * Tap the circle: from 1 to 5 stars the rating goes to 0 stars; from
    0 stars it goes to unrated.

Lock screen and notification
----------------------------

Two buttons change the rating by one step:

    one star more:  unrated -> 0 stars -> 1 star -> ... -> 5 stars
                    (no change at 5 stars)
    one star less:  5 stars -> ... -> 1 star -> 0 stars -> unrated
                    (no change when unrated)

The button descriptions name the current rating in the same form:
"One star more (3 *)".

Storage and sync
----------------

A rating is written to the phone's tags.db at once, with the time of the
change.  It reaches the desktop at the next sync, where the newer of
the desktop's and the phone's change wins for each song (11).

If the phone's database is from an older version, rating fails with
"The database is from an older version: sync with the desktop to rate
tracks."  Sync once and rate again.

Rating what was played: the Recent tab
--------------------------------------

Songs often cannot be rated while driving.  The third tab, "Recent", lists
the songs that were played lately, the latest first, to be looked over and
rated afterwards:

  * "24 hours", "3 days" and "Week" choose how far back to look.
  * "Not rated" (on at first) leaves out the songs that already have a
    rating, so the list is what still needs one.
  * A row shows the title, the artist and album, when the song was played
    ("Today  2:15 PM", "Yesterday ...", or the day of the week) and five
    stars.  Tap star N to give N stars; tapping the star that is already the
    rating sets 0 stars.  A rating is written at once, as for the playing
    song, and the row shows it at once.  A row stays in the list until the
    list is made again (a filter is changed, or the tab is opened again).
  * The copies of a song (the same artist and title) are one row.  The
    plays are those of the whole song (see above), so a copy that the phone
    did not play can show up; the rating goes to the copy that the row shows.
  * At most 300 songs are shown.


Using ratings
-------------

Dynamic playlists made on the desktop can select by rating (05); the
phone plays them like other playlists.  Use "Rating is not set" to list
unrated songs, or "Rating = 0" for songs rated 0 stars.
